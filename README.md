# Privacy Policy

WeCampus respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you choose to share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information WeCampus collects, how we use it, how it may be shared, and what rights users have regarding their data.

By using WeCampus, creating an account, or submitting information through the application, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

WeCampus collects only the information necessary to operate, secure, and improve the application.

### Account Information

When you create or use an account, WeCampus may collect information such as:

* Name and surname
* Email address
* Username or profile identifier
* Profile photo, if voluntarily uploaded
* School, university, education level, or other educational background information, if voluntarily provided
* Authentication information provided through email login or third-party sign-in services such as Google Sign-In

### User-Provided Information

WeCampus allows users to voluntarily provide and share information within the application. This may include:

* Profile details
* Posts, comments, and other shared content
* Group chat messages or program-related communication
* Daily activities, experiences, résumé/CV details, achievements, and educational interests
* Program applications, application answers, participation details, and program-related submissions

Providing this information is optional unless it is required for a specific feature, such as submitting a program application. Information that users choose to publish or submit may be visible to other users, organizations, or program owners depending on the feature being used.

### Program and Participation Data

When users interact with programs, events, or opportunities on WeCampus, the app may process data such as:

* Programs viewed, saved, liked, or applied to
* Application status and submitted answers
* Program participation records
* QR-based check-in or attendance records, when used by a program organizer
* Certificates, credentials, or participation records issued through WeCampus

This information is used to operate program discovery, applications, participation tracking, and related platform features.

### Media, Camera, and Photo Library Access

WeCampus does not require users to upload photos to use the application. However, users may choose to upload profile images, post images, or program-related media.

WeCampus may request access to the camera or photo library only when users choose to upload media, update their profile image, share content, or use QR-based check-in features. These permissions are optional and are used only for the feature selected by the user.

WeCampus does not continuously access the camera and does not use the microphone unless a future feature clearly requests and explains that permission.

### Notifications and Device Information

If users allow notifications, WeCampus may collect and store device notification tokens in order to send relevant app notifications, such as program updates, application results, reminders, announcements, or safety-related messages.

WeCampus may also process limited technical information necessary for app security, debugging, performance, authentication, and service reliability.

### Location Information

WeCampus does not request or track precise device location. Users may voluntarily provide location-related information, such as school, city, program location, or event location, when using specific features.

### Sensitive Data

WeCampus does not intentionally collect financial information, health data, contact lists, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal data unless a user voluntarily includes such information in content they submit. Users should avoid sharing sensitive personal information in posts, comments, messages, applications, or profile fields unless it is strictly necessary.

## 2. How We Use Information

WeCampus may use collected information to:

* Create and manage user accounts
* Provide profile, feed, post, comment, chat, program, application, and discovery features
* Allow users to apply to programs and manage participation
* Enable organizations to manage applications, participants, announcements, schedules, attendance, and certificates
* Send important notifications, such as application results or program updates
* Improve app safety, reliability, performance, and user experience
* Detect, prevent, and respond to spam, abuse, policy violations, or security issues
* Review reports of objectionable content or abusive users
* Provide support and respond to user requests
* Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use and community guidelines

## 3. User-Generated Content and Visibility

WeCampus includes user-generated content features such as profiles, posts, comments, program interactions, and group chat areas.

Content that users choose to share may be visible to other users, organizations, program owners, or relevant participants depending on the feature and privacy settings available in the app.

Users are responsible for the information they choose to share. Users should not post private, sensitive, or confidential information that they do not want others to see.

WeCampus may review, remove, restrict, or moderate user-generated content that violates our Terms of Use, User-Generated Content Guidelines, safety rules, or applicable laws.

## 4. Reporting, Blocking, and Moderation

WeCampus provides safety tools that allow users to report inappropriate content or abusive behavior. Users may also block other users where supported by the app.

Reports may be reviewed by the WeCampus team to help enforce platform rules, protect users, and prevent misuse. When necessary, WeCampus may remove content, limit visibility, suspend accounts, or take other appropriate action.

## 5. Data Sharing

WeCampus does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties for marketing or commercial purposes.

WeCampus may share or process data with trusted service providers only as necessary to operate the application. These providers may include:

* Supabase, for backend infrastructure, authentication, database, and storage services
* Google Sign-In, if the user chooses to sign in with Google
* Expo or notification service providers, for push notification delivery
* Other technical service providers required for hosting, security, analytics, debugging, or app functionality

These providers may process data only as needed to provide their services to WeCampus.

WeCampus may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, safety obligations, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, WeCampus, or others.

## 6. Data Security

WeCampus takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no online service can guarantee complete security. Users should protect their account credentials and avoid sharing sensitive information through public or social features.

## 7. Data Retention

WeCampus retains user information only for as long as necessary to provide the application, operate user accounts, maintain program records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and enforce platform rules.

Some records, such as moderation logs, safety reports, application records, attendance records, certificates, or audit records, may be retained where necessary for platform integrity, legal compliance, or legitimate operational purposes.

## 8. User Rights and Data Deletion

Users may access, update, or delete certain personal information within the application where available.

Users may also request account or data deletion by contacting WeCampus at:

[wecampus.support@gmail.com](mailto:wecampus.support@gmail.com)

When a deletion request is received, WeCampus will process the request in accordance with applicable laws and platform requirements. Some information may be retained where necessary for legal, security, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate recordkeeping purposes.

## 9. Children and Teen Users

WeCampus is not directed at children under the age of 13. Users under the age of 13 may not create an account or use WeCampus.

Because WeCampus is designed for students, including teen users, we aim to limit data collection to what is necessary, provide safety and moderation tools, and maintain protections against objectionable content and abusive behavior.

If you believe that a child under the age of 13 has provided personal information to WeCampus, please contact us at [wecampus.support@gmail.com](mailto:wecampus.support@gmail.com) so we can review and take appropriate action.

WeCampus may display links to external programs, events, organizations, websites, or application forms. WeCampus is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or data handling of third-party websites or services.

Users should review the privacy policies of any third-party services they choose to access.

WeCampus may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we may update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify users through the app or other reasonable means.

Continued use of WeCampus after updates means that users accept the updated Privacy Policy.
